Top Jaipur Sightseeing Places Covered

Our drivers take you directly to Pink City's famous UNESCO World Heritage forts & palaces.

Amber Fort & Sheesh Mahal

Majestic hilltop palace featuring intricate mirror work, elephant rides, and Maota lake view.

Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)

Iconic 5-storey honeycomb pink sandstone facade with 953 ornate latticed windows.

Jal Mahal (Water Palace)

Picturesque palace submerged in the middle of Man Sagar Lake in Jaipur.

City Palace & Chandra Mahal

Royal residence of Jaipur Maharajas blending Mughal, Rajput and European architecture.
